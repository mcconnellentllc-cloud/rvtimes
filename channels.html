How to use this

Type any part of a channel name (HBO, HGTV, History) or a number (89) to filter. On the SuperBOX remote, use the search button for the same thing on the TV. The box carries about 1,994 channels and new ones get added here as EPG screenshots come in.

Growing the list

This list is seeded from the EPG photos on hand. To add more: on the SuperBOX, scroll through each category (Network TV, Regional Locals, Sports Networks, 24/7, PPV Events, MLS) and snap a photo of the channel list. The site admin adds them to the database and they show up here.

Thermostat

Duo-Therm Comfort Control · heat & air
Where it is

On the ceiling in the walkway, toward the front of the coach. Two zones: Zone 1 is the living area, Zone 2 is the bedroom.

Buttons on the thermostat
  • MODE Cycles OFF › FURNACE › COOL › HEAT+COOL. Keep tapping until the display shows what you want.
  • ZONE Picks Zone 1 (living) or Zone 2 (bedroom). You can set each zone to a different temperature.
  • ▲ ▼ Sets the target temperature (set point) for the selected zone.
  • FAN AUTO (fan runs only with heat/cool) or ON (fan runs all the time).
To run the heat (furnace)
  1. Make sure there is propane in the tank. Check the LP level on the bathroom panel — hold TEST, read the P / LP row.
  2. Press MODE until the display shows FURNACE (or FURN).
  3. Press ZONE and pick the zone you want warm. Set both zones separately if you want the whole coach warm.
  4. Use ▲ to raise the set point above the current room temperature.
  5. The furnace kicks on with a whoosh. Warm air comes out of the floor vents. Give it 20–30 minutes to feel the coach warm up. The furnace burns propane, so run times matter.
To run the air conditioning
Power first

The roof A/C units run on 120 V shore power or the generator. If nothing is plugged in and the generator is off, the A/C will not come on no matter what the thermostat says.

  1. Confirm 50 A shore power is live, or start the generator. On 30 A hookup, you can usually run one roof A/C, not both.
  2. Press MODE until the display shows COOL.
  3. Press ZONE and pick the zone to cool.
  4. Use ▼ to drop the set point below the current room temperature.
  5. The roof A/C kicks on. Cold air comes out of the ceiling vents. Close the roof vents and windows so the A/C isn’t fighting the outside.
If it isn't working
Heat won't light

Nine times out of ten it's propane. Check the LP level on the bathroom panel. Also check that the propane valve on the coach is open.

A/C won't come on

Check the power source: is the shore cord plugged in? Is the generator running? Also check the breakers in the electrical panel inside the coach.

Takes a while

Either mode takes a solid 20–30 minutes to make the coach comfortable. Don't crank the set point way past what you actually want — it doesn't heat or cool any faster, and you'll overshoot.

Tank & LPG Levels

KIB panel · bathroom backsplash
The four tank rows (left side)
  • LPG Propane — the on-board LP tank. Feeds the furnace, water heater, stove and oven.
  • FRESH Fresh water — drinking water in the holding tank. Refill when it gets low.
  • HOLDING Black water (the toilet tank). Dump when 2/3 or F.
  • GRAY Gray water — sink and shower drain. Dump after black so the gray flushes the sewer hose.
Battery column (right side)
  • C Charging — the coach is plugged in or the engine/generator is running.
  • G Good — batteries are at a healthy charge.
  • F Fair — getting low, plan to charge soon.
  • L Low — plug in or start the generator before things start shutting off.
How to read a level
  1. Press and hold the TEST button (bottom left). The LEDs only light while you're holding it — this saves the sensor.
  2. Look at each column. The highest LED that lights tells you how full that tank is. Levels bottom to top: E › 1/3 › 2/3 › F (full).
The three switches (below the LEDs)
  • TEST Hold it to read the levels.
  • PUMP Turns the fresh-water pump ON/OFF. Pump must be ON for the sinks, shower and toilet to run when the coach is not hooked up to city water.
  • HEATER Turns the water heater ON/OFF.
When to dump the holding tanks
Black (HOLDING) first, then GRAY

Open the black (HOLDING) valve first and let it drain completely. Close it. Then open the gray valve — the gray water rinses the sewer hose on its way out.

After the dump

Add a couple of gallons of fresh water and a tank treatment packet to the black tank. Keeps solids from sticking to the probes and keeps the smell down.

When to refill fresh water
Fresh fill

When FRESH reads 1/3 or lower. The fresh water fill is a connection on the passenger side. Use a drinking-water-rated hose.

When to refill propane
LP tank is permanent

The LP tank is built into the coach — it's not a removable bottle. When LPG reads 1/3 or lower, take the coach to a propane filling station. Cold weather burns propane fast because the furnace and water heater both run on it.

If a tank reads wrong
Fouled probes

Black-tank (HOLDING) probes get fouled over time — toilet paper and solids stick to them and the panel reads full even after a dump. This is a known RV quirk, not a broken panel. Flush with water and a tank cleaner to clear them.

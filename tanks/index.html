Tank & LPG Levels

KIB panel · bathroom backsplash
Where it is

Small black panel on the bathroom backsplash above the sink. Four rows of LEDs with a TEST button at the bottom.

How to read it
  1. Press and hold the TEST button. The LEDs only light while you're holding it — this saves the batteries.
  2. Read each row. The highest LED that's lit tells you how full that tank is. E = empty, then 1/3, 2/3, F = full. Some panels show 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, F.
The four rows
  • F Fresh water — drinking water in the holding tank. Refill when it gets to 1/4.
  • G Gray water — sink and shower drain. Dump when 3/4 or full.
  • B Black water — toilet drain. Dump before gray so the gray water flushes the sewer hose.
  • LP Propane — the on-board LP tank. Fills the furnace, water heater, stove, oven.
When to dump the holding tanks
Black first, then gray

Open the black valve first and let it drain completely. Close it. Then open the gray valve — the gray water rinses the sewer hose on its way out.

After the dump

Add a couple of gallons of fresh water and a tank treatment packet to the black tank. Keeps solids from sticking to the probes and keeps the smell down.

When to refill fresh water
Fresh fill

When F reads 1/4 or lower. The fresh water fill is a connection on the passenger side. Use a drinking-water-rated hose.

When to refill propane
LP tank is permanent

The LP tank is built into the coach — it's not a removable bottle. When LP reads 1/4 or lower, take the coach to a propane filling station. Cold weather burns propane fast because the furnace and water heater both run on it.

If a tank reads wrong
Fouled probes

Black tank probes get fouled over time — toilet paper and solids stick to them and the panel reads full even after a dump. This is a known RV quirk, not a broken panel. Flush with water and a tank cleaner to clear them.

What this box is

The SuperBOX S3 Pro is a streaming / IPTV box. It carries essentially every US cable network, premium movie service, and major sporting event — 1,994 live channels in the EPG and roughly 10,000 VOD titles. It runs on Android (Google's TV OS), so the Browser tile and the YouTube app work like they would on any other Android device — search for anything, Google questions, pull up a game on YouTube if a channel's being flaky.

Quick orientation

Front Vizio TV › switch input to HDMI 2. Remote asleep? Slide the power switch on the top-right edge OFF then ON. Full text references in Knowledge.

How to Use the TV

Front Vizio · SuperBOX streamer
Three things to know

Front TV input is HDMI 2. Remote asleep? Slide the power switch on the top-right edge OFF then ON. The two left-side buttons are left-click and back like a mouse.

Getting a picture
  1. Turn on the front Vizio TV with the Vizio remote.
  2. Press INPUT on the Vizio remote. Pick HDMI 2. That's the SuperBOX feed. HDMI 1 and the other inputs go to the other devices.
  3. You should now see the SuperBOX home screen (Live TV, VOD, Browser, etc.).
The SuperBOX remote
If the remote is dead

Slide the little power switch on the top-right edge of the remote to OFF, wait a second, then back ON. The light on the front should blink. That wakes it up.

  • Left btn Left mouse click — click on whatever the pointer is over.
  • Right btn Back — goes back one screen, or exits an app.
  • Arrows Move the cursor / scroll through channels.
  • OK Enter / tune the highlighted channel.
  • SEARCH Find a channel by typing part of its name.
  • HOME Back to the SuperBOX main menu.
Live TV (cable channels)
  1. From the home screen, click LIVE TV.
  2. Scroll with the arrows. Press OK on a channel to tune it. Press the left arrow from the channel list to open category types: Network TV, Regional Locals, Sports, 24/7, PPV, MLS.
  3. Can't find a channel? Press SEARCH and type part of the name (HBO, ESPN, History).
Video on Demand (movies & shows)
  1. From the home screen, click VOD.
  2. Browse by genre or press SEARCH to find a title.
  3. Click the title and pick a stream. If one won't play, back out and try another source.
The Browser (Google & YouTube)
It's an Android device

The SuperBOX runs on Android, so there's a Browser tile and a YouTube app on the home screen. Search for anything, pull up a game on YouTube if a channel is being flaky, Google a question. Works like a phone.

Common gotchas
No picture

Nine times out of ten the TV is on the wrong input. Press INPUT on the Vizio remote and pick HDMI 2.

Remote doesn’t do anything

Slide the power switch on the top-right edge of the remote OFF then ON. If still dead, the SuperBOX itself may be asleep — unplug its power cord for 10 seconds and plug it back in.

Channel buffering or stuck

Press the right-button (back) and tune a different channel. If several channels are buffering, the internet may be slow — check Starlink. Last resort: YouTube or the Browser tile.

How to Use the TV

Front Vizio · SuperBOX streamer
Three things to know

Front TV input is HDMI 2. Remote is a mini wireless keyboard with a touchpad, not a standard remote. The L and R marks on the touchpad are left-click and right-click; right-click is back.

Getting a picture
  1. Turn on the front Vizio TV with the Vizio remote.
  2. Press INPUT on the Vizio remote. Pick HDMI 2. That's the SuperBOX feed. HDMI 1 and the other inputs go elsewhere.
  3. You should see the SuperBOX home screen (LIVE TV, VOD, Browser, etc.) exactly like the photo above.
The remote
If the remote is dead

Slide the little power switch on the top-right edge of the remote to OFF, wait a second, then back ON. The blue LED on the front blinks when it wakes up.

  • Touchpad Big black pad in the middle. Slide a finger to move the cursor around the screen.
  • L Left mouse button (bottom-left of the touchpad, or the standalone left side) — click what the pointer is over.
  • R Right mouse button (right of the touchpad) — back, or exit an app.
  • D-pad & OK Round pad on the right with four arrows and OK in the middle. Scroll through lists and press OK to pick.
  • Home House icon above the D-pad — jumps back to the SuperBOX main menu.
  • QWERTY keys Type to search for channels and shows.
  • V+ / V− Media volume up/down on the top-left.
The home-screen tiles
  • LIVE TV Cable channels — 1,994 of them.
  • VOD Video on demand — movies and shows.
  • Playback Replay what you recently watched.
  • Browser Web browser. Google anything, pull up YouTube, whatever.
  • Apps Other streaming apps installed on the box.
  • App Store Install more apps. You usually won't need this.
  • YouTube The YouTube app — direct link, no browser.
Live TV (cable channels)
  1. From the home screen, click LIVE TV.
  2. Scroll the channel list with the arrows. Press OK on a channel to tune it. The preview plays in the top-right pane. Press the left arrow to jump to the category list (ALL, Favorite, Network TV, Regional Locals, Sports, 24/7, PPV, MLS).
  3. Click OK on the channel to make it full-screen.
Find a channel by name
  1. From the channel list, press the search (magnifying glass) icon on the D-pad.
  2. Type the name — HBO, ESPN, History — and the matching channels show up with their numbers.
  3. Click the one you want. You can also search on this site at Channels if you're not at the TV.
Video on Demand (movies & shows)
  1. From the home screen, click VOD.
  2. Browse by genre or press the search icon to find a title.
  3. Click the title and pick a stream. If one won't play, back out (right mouse button) and try another source.
The Browser (Google & YouTube)
It's an Android device

The SuperBOX runs on Android, so there's a Browser tile and a YouTube tile. Search for anything, pull up a game on YouTube if a channel is being flaky, Google a question. Works like a phone. Use the QWERTY keys on the remote to type.

Common gotchas
No picture

Nine times out of ten the TV is on the wrong input. Press INPUT on the Vizio remote and pick HDMI 2.

Remote doesn’t do anything

Slide the power switch on the top-right edge of the remote OFF then ON. If still dead, the SuperBOX itself may be asleep — unplug its power cord for 10 seconds and plug it back in.

Channel buffering or stuck

Right-click (back) and tune a different channel. If several channels are buffering, the internet may be slow — check Starlink. Last resort: YouTube or the Browser tile.

Water Heater — How To Turn It On

No hot water? Check these FIRST. 1. Is the HEATER switch actually ON at the KIB panel? (Red light showing.) 2. Has it been on for 20–30 minutes? Cold-start to shower-hot takes that long on 6 gallons.
  1. Propane ON at the tank — passenger-side bay P3.
  2. Fresh water tank has water, OR city water is hooked up at the wet bay (driver-side D4). Never fire with no water.
  3. On the KIB panel, flip the HEATER switch ON.
  4. A RED LIGHT comes on — ignition is cycling.
  5. Listen for a click, then a steady burn.
  6. Walk to the CLOSET, open it, look down at the unit, and confirm the burner is actually lit through the sight port.
  7. Wait 20–30 minutes for 6 gallons to come up to temperature. The heater will then cycle on and off to hold temp.
Walk to the closet and confirm the flame. Flipping the switch starts the ignition — it doesn't guarantee the heater actually lit. Unlit cycling means unburned propane is venting. Confirm the flame every time.

To turn OFF

  • Flip the HEATER switch OFF at the KIB panel. The red light goes out.
  • Before driving or long storage, also close propane at the P3 tank.

KIB Monitor Panel — Check Tank & Battery Levels

The gauges are dark when idle. Press and hold the TEST switch to light them up — the longer bars = fuller tank. Release when done so the house battery isn't being drained to light a readout.

GaugeWhat it shows
LPGPropane tank (P3). Check before cooking, heating, or lighting the water heater.
FRESHFresh drinking / washing water tank.
HOLDINGBlack water — the toilet tank. Dump before it's full.
GRAYGray water — sinks and shower.
BATTERYHouse battery. L = Low · F = Fair · G = Good · C = Charging (over 13 V on shore / solar / generator).

Water Pump Switch

ON

Fresh water pump runs. Use this when boondocking — you're drawing from the FRESH tank.

OFF

You're on city water at the wet bay (D4). Pump off so it doesn't dry-run while city pressure feeds the coach.

If Something's Wrong

SymptomMost likely cause
Water heater clicks but won't lightPropane off at P3, tank empty, or igniter needs another cycle. HEATER off, wait 30 sec, HEATER on.
No click at all when HEATER is on12 V to the heater is dead. Check fuse / breaker in D1; confirm the KIB switch moves freely.
No hot water, but HEATER has been onDid someone flip it off? And has it had 20–30 minutes? Those are the usual answers before anything else is actually wrong.
Burns briefly then shuts offFlame sensor dirty or exhaust blocked. Pull the exterior access cover and inspect.
Heater burns but water stays coldBypass valve left in WINTERIZE mode. Behind the closet, at the top of the unit — return to NORMAL.
No water pressure anywherePUMP switch is off (if boondocking), fresh tank empty, or city hose kinked / disconnected.

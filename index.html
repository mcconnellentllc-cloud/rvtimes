What this site is

Instructions for the three things people ask about most in this coach: the thermostat, the tank levels, and the TV. Each of the laminated cards in the coach has a QR code that drops you straight into the page you need.

The three cards in the coach
Thermostat Heat & air · Duo-Therm Comfort Control › Tank & LPG Levels Bathroom panel · fresh, gray, black, LP › How to Use the TV HDMI 2 · SuperBOX remote · channels ›
How to use this site
  • Scan Point your phone camera at a QR code on one of the laminated cards. It opens the matching page.
  • Tap Open a page by tapping a card above. Tap the back arrow on your phone to come back here.
  • Steps Tap a step to check it off. Checks are remembered on your phone.
  • Menu Swipe the top navigation strip left and right to see every section.
